## 1. GIOI THIEU CHUNG

He thong quan ly cua hang sua chua giup nhan vien quay:
- **Tiep nhan** thiet bi cua khach hang mot cach nhanh chong
- **Tao va theo doi phieu sua chua** theo thoi gian thuc
- **Quan ly thong tin khach hang** va lich su sua chua
- **Ban giao thiet bi, lap hoa don** va thu tien
- **Tra cuu bao hanh** khi khach hang quay lai

> **Luu y:** Tai khoan nhan vien quay chi co quyen **STAFF**.
> Cac chuc nang quan ly kho, bao cao doanh thu chi tiet do Admin quan ly.

## 2. DANG NHAP HE THONG

### 2.1. Cac buoc dang nhap
1. Mo trinh duyet web va truy cap dia chi he thong (do quan ly cung cap).
2. Nhap **Ten dang nhap** va **Mat khau** vao o tuong ung.
3. Nhan nut **"Dang nhap"**.
4. He thong chuyen den trang Dashboard neu dang nhap thanh cong.

### 2.2. Quen mat khau
Lien he quan ly hoac bo phan ky thuat de duoc cap lai mat khau.
Nhan vien quay **khong tu dat lai mat khau** duoc.

### 2.3. Dang xuat
Nhan vao ten tai khoan goc phai tren cung chon **"Dang xuat"**.
> CANH BAO: **Hay dang xuat khi roi khoi may tinh** de bao mat thong tin khach hang.

## 4. QUAN LY KHACH HANG

### 4.1. Tim kiem khach hang
1. Vao menu **"Khach hang"** > **"Danh sach khach hang"**.
2. Nhap so dien thoai hoac ten khach vao o tim kiem.
3. Nhan Enter hoac nut tim kiem.
4. Click vao ten khach de xem chi tiet (lich su phieu, thiet bi da sua).

### 4.2. Them khach hang moi
1. Nhan nut **"+ Them khach hang"**.
2. Dien day du thong tin:

| Truong | Bat buoc | Ghi chu |
|---|---|---|
| Ho va ten | Co | Ten day du |
| So dien thoai | Co | Dung de tra cuu nhanh |
| Email | Khong | De gui thong bao |
| Dia chi | Khong | Ghi neu khach yeu cau giao nhan |
| Ghi chu | Khong | Thong tin dac biet ve khach |

3. Nhan **"Luu"**. He thong tu tao ma khach hang (VD: KH000125).

### 4.3. Chinh sua thong tin khach
1. Tim khach > nhan nut **"Sua"**.
2. Cap nhat thong tin > nhan **"Luu"**.

> CANH BAO: **Khong duoc xoa khach hang** da co lich su phieu. Neu can, bao admin.

## 5. TIEP NHAN THIET BI - TAO PHIEU SUA CHUA

### 5.1. Quy trinh tiep nhan
```
Khach den > Tim/Tao khach hang > Tao phieu sua chua > In phieu giao khach
```

### 5.2. Cac buoc tao phieu sua chua

**Buoc 1: Chon khach hang**
1. Vao **"Phieu sua chua"** > nhan **"+ Tao phieu moi"**.
2. Go so dien thoai vao o tim kiem khach hang.
3. Neu co khach cu > chon tu danh sach.
4. Neu chua co > nhan **"Tao khach moi"** va dien thong tin.

**Buoc 2: Chon/Them thiet bi**
1. Danh sach thiet bi cu cua khach hien ra.
2. Neu thiet bi cu > chon tu danh sach.
3. Neu thiet bi moi > nhan **"+ Them thiet bi"** va dien:

| Truong | Bat buoc | Vi du |
|---|---|---|
| Loai thiet bi | Co | Dien thoai, Laptop, May in |
| Thuong hieu | Khong | Samsung, Dell, HP |
| Model | Khong | Galaxy S23, Latitude 5520 |
| So serial | Khong | Ghi de phan biet thiet bi |

**Buoc 3: Dien thong tin phieu**

| Truong | Bat buoc | Ghi chu |
|---|---|---|
| Loai dich vu | Co | Chon tu danh muc |
| Tinh trang thiet bi luc nhan | Co | Mo ta chi tiet: tray xuoc, vo man... |
| Yeu cau cua khach | Co | Khach muon lam gi |
| Gia tien cong du kien | Khong | Dien neu da thong nhat voi khach |
| Gia linh kien du kien | Khong | Dien neu da thong nhat voi khach |
| Ngay du kien tra | Khong | Hen ngay cho khach |

**Buoc 4: Luu va in phieu**
1. Kiem tra lai toan bo thong tin.
2. Nhan **"Tao phieu"**. Ma phieu tu dong tao (VD: PSC2026100001).
3. Nhan **"In phieu tiep nhan"** de in va giao cho khach.

> BAT BUOC giao phieu tiep nhan cho khach - khach can phieu nay de lay thiet bi.

### 5.3. Phan cong ky thuat vien
1. Mo phieu vua tao > nhan **"Phan cong KTV"**.
2. Chon ky thuat vien phu hop.
3. Nhan **"Xac nhan"**. Trang thai phieu chuyen sang **DIAGNOSING**.

---

## 6. THEO DOI TRANG THAI PHIEU

### 6.1. So do trang thai phieu
```
RECEIVED > DIAGNOSING > WAITING_CONFIRM > REPAIRING > COMPLETED > DELIVERED
                                                             |
                                                        CANCELLED
```

| Trang thai | Y nghia | Hanh dong NV Quay |
|---|---|---|
| RECEIVED | Moi tiep nhan, chua phan cong | Phan cong KTV |
| DIAGNOSING | KTV dang kiem tra may | Cho KTV bao ket qua |
| WAITING_CONFIRM | Cho khach xac nhan bao gia | **Goi dien cho khach** |
| REPAIRING | Dang sua chua | Cho |
| COMPLETED | Sua xong, cho ban giao | Bao khach den lay |
| DELIVERED | Da ban giao, hoan tat | Luu tru |
| CANCELLED | Huy phieu | Ghi ro ly do |

### 6.2. Goi khach xac nhan bao gia
Khi phieu o trang thai **WAITING_CONFIRM**:
1. Goi dien cho khach theo so duoc luu.
2. Thong bao chi phi tien cong + linh kien uoc tinh.
3. Khach dong y > vao phieu > nhan **"Xac nhan sua chua"** > chuyen thanh REPAIRING.
4. Khach khong dong y > nhan **"Huy phieu"** > dien ly do > in phieu tra may.

---

## 7. BAN GIAO THIET BI VA THU TIEN

### 7.1. Khi khach den lay may

**Buoc 1: Xac minh khach hang**
1. Yeu cau khach xuat trinh phieu tiep nhan.
2. Tim phieu theo ma hoac SDT khach.
3. Kiem tra khop thong tin (ten khach, thiet bi).

**Buoc 2: Thu tien**
1. Mo phieu > nhan **"Tao hoa don"**.
2. Kiem tra lai: Tien cong + Tien linh kien - Giam gia = **Tong thanh toan**.
3. Chon hinh thuc thanh toan: Tien mat hoac Chuyen khoan.
4. Nhan **"Xac nhan thanh toan"** > trang thai hoa don chuyen thanh PAID.

**Buoc 3: Ban giao thiet bi**
1. Giao thiet bi cho khach.
2. Nhan **"Xac nhan ban giao"** > trang thai phieu chuyen thanh DELIVERED.
3. Nhan **"In hoa don"** de in va giao cho khach.

> He thong tu dong tao phieu bao hanh neu dich vu co thoi han bao hanh.

---

## 8. TRA CUU MA BAO HANH

Khi khach quay lai yeu cau bao hanh:
1. Vao **"Bao hanh"** > **"Tra cuu bao hanh"**.
2. Nhap ma bao hanh (VD: BH2026100001) hoac SDT khach.
3. Kiem tra trang thai:

| Trang thai BH | Y nghia |
|---|---|
| ACTIVE | Con hieu luc, duoc sua mien phi |
| EXPIRED | Da het han, tinh phi binh thuong |
| VOID | Bi huy (do khach tu sua noi khac) |
| USED | Da dung 1 lan bao hanh |

4. Neu con han > bao ky thuat vien kiem tra > tao phieu sua bao hanh moi.

---

## 9. CAC LUU Y VA TINH HUONG THUONG GAP

**Khach mat phieu tiep nhan:**
Tim phieu bang SDT. Xac minh them bang ten khach va thong tin thiet bi truoc khi giao.

**Khach yeu cau huy phieu khi dang sua:**
Lien he KTV de dung sua chua. Vao phieu > "Huy phieu" > dien ly do.
Thu phi chan doan neu co theo quy dinh cua hang.

**Nhap nham thong tin phieu:**
Chi co the sua phieu khi trang thai la RECEIVED hoac DIAGNOSING.
Khi da sang REPAIRING tro di, can bao admin.

**Khach muon lay may nhung chua sua xong:**
Vao phieu > "Huy phieu" > chon ly do "Khach lay lai may chua sua". In bien ban giao tra.

**May sua xong nhung khach chua den lay nhieu ngay:**
Goi dien nhac khach. Ghi chu vao Note cua phieu.
Bao quan ly neu sau 7 ngay khach van khong den.
